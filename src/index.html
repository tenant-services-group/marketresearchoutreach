Market Research Outreach

Extract contacts from a property PDF, review and finalize the list, then generate one draft email per contact.

1 Get your contact list

Extract contacts straight from a property PDF report, or upload an Excel file you already have.

2 Review & finalize contacts

Type directly into any cell to fix or fill in what the PDF didn't capture. Missing first names and emails are highlighted. Rows without an email are left out when you finalize.

3 Choose your email template

Use NAME where the contact's first name goes and ADDRESS where the address list goes — both are replaced automatically for each contact. Everything else is kept exactly as written.

4 Choose what goes in the subject line

5 Generate

This builds one draft per unique email address, combining every property address tied to that contact.
